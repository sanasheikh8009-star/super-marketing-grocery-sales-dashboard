    Data notes

    Source: Supermart grocery sales file, 9,994 orders, cleaned with scripts/clean_data.py. Money is shown in rupees (the file does not state a currency; INR is assumed).

    • About 4,000 order dates had day and month swapped in the source file. They were corrected before any number on this page was calculated.
    • The Region column is not tied to a city: every city appears in several regions. Region and city results are therefore separate views, not nested.
    • Region "North" holds a single order, so its figures are not meaningful.
    • All orders are in Tamil Nadu, so state-level comparison is not possible. Locations are compared at city level.
    • "High sales, low profit" means: sales at or above the median of the items shown and profit margin below their median margin.
